# Privacy Policy — Roye’s Meso Tracker

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

## Overview

Roye’s Meso Tracker is a browser extension that helps players track item sales and locations on [meso.watch](https://meso.watch/), receive sound notifications about new sales, and configure price thresholds using in-game currency.

## Information We Collect

Roye’s Meso Tracker does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the extension.

The extension stores certain preferences and tracking settings locally in the browser. It also uses browser storage to remember whether the extension is enabled.

## Website Data

The extension reads market data made available by meso.watch to provide its tracking features. Users must access meso.watch for the extension to function.

Roye’s Meso Tracker is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the operators of meso.watch.

## Data Sharing

The extension is not designed to sell users’ personal information or share their tracking preferences with advertisers.

## Data Retention and Control

Settings saved locally remain in the browser until users change or delete them, clear the relevant website or extension data, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling the extension may not automatically remove data saved separately by the website.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through the contact details listed on the Roye’s Meso Tracker Chrome Web Store listing.
